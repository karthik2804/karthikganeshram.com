Creamy kimchi and sesame udon

Tangy kimchi udon with a creamy silken tofu and tahini sauce, topped with a fried egg.

Cook
10 min
Yield
1 serving

What goes in

Ingredients

  • 100 g dry udon noodles
  • 150 g silken tofu
  • 50–60 g kimchi without fish sauce or shrimp paste, chopped
  • 1 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 2 tsp tahini
  • ½ tsp red miso
  • ½–1 tsp gochujang
  • ½ tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp sesame oil
  • Noodle water, as needed
  • 1 egg
  • A little neutral oil, for frying the egg
  • Spring onions, sliced, to finish
  • Sesame seeds, to finish

Kimchi brings the tang; silken tofu and tahini make the sauce creamy and nutty.

Method

  1. Cook the udon according to the packet instructions. Save a little noodle water before draining.
  2. Blend the silken tofu, tahini, miso, gochujang, soy sauce, and kimchi juice until smooth.
  3. Heat the sesame oil in a pan and briefly sauté the chopped kimchi. Add the blended sauce and warm it gently.
  4. Toss in the udon, adding noodle water a little at a time until the sauce coats the noodles. Meanwhile, fry the egg in a little oil in another pan.
  5. Top the noodles with the fried egg, spring onions, and sesame seeds.

Notes

The kimchi juice and noodle water will change the balance each time, so add a little more of either to taste.
