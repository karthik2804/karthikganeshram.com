Air fryer

Soft and hard air fryer eggs

Jammy or hard-set eggs in the air fryer, with the time and temperature for each.

Prep
2 min
Cook
10–12 min
Yield
1–4 eggs

What goes in

Ingredients

  • 1–4 eggs
  • A bowl of ice water

I use the air fryer when I want an egg for toast, noodles, or a quick snack. The timing changes with the egg and the air fryer, so these are starting points.

Method

  1. Put the eggs into the air fryer basket in a single layer.
  2. For a soft, jammy yolk, cook at 120°C for 10 minutes. For a hard-set yolk, cook at 130°C for 12 minutes.
  3. Transfer the eggs to ice water right away for about 5 minutes to stop them cooking.
