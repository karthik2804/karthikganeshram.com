Kimchi egg noodles

Quick egg noodles with caramelised kimchi, soy sauce, mirin, and a fried egg.

Prep
5 min
Cook
15 min
Yield
1 serving

What goes in

Ingredients

  • 90–100 g dry egg noodles
  • 100–120 g kimchi without fish sauce or shrimp paste, roughly chopped
  • 1–2 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 egg
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • Sesame seeds, to finish
  • Neutral oil, for frying

A quick bowl with caramelised kimchi, glossy noodles, and a fried egg on top.

Method

  1. Cook the egg noodles according to the packet instructions. Save a little noodle water before draining.
  2. Heat a little neutral oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the kimchi and fry for 4–5 minutes, until lightly caramelised.
  3. Stir in the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Add the kimchi juice, soy sauce, and mirin, then simmer briefly.
  4. Add the drained noodles with a splash of noodle water. Toss until glossy and evenly coated.
  5. Fry the egg separately, keeping the yolk runny if you like.
  6. Drizzle the noodles with sesame oil and top with the egg, spring onion, and sesame seeds.

Notes

For a more filling bowl, add 150–200 g crispy tofu and some sautéed mushrooms. I adjust the kimchi juice and noodle water by feel, depending on the kimchi.
