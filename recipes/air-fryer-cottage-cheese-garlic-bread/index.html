Cottage cheese garlic bread

A cottage cheese and gram flour bread with garlic and cheese inside.

Prep
10 min
Cook
10–12 min
Yield
1 filled bread

What goes in

Ingredients

Dough

  • 50 g cottage cheese
  • 60 g gram flour (besan)
  • A pinch of baking soda

Filling

  • Garlic, finely chopped, to taste
  • Grated cheese, to taste
  • Jalapeños, finely chopped (optional)

I make the bread from cottage cheese and gram flour, then tuck the garlic and cheese inside before air frying it.

Method

  1. Mix the cottage cheese, gram flour, and baking soda into a dough. If it feels too dry to come together, add a little water; if sticky, dust it with a little more gram flour.
  2. Roll the dough out into a small flat bread. Put the garlic and grated cheese in the middle, adding jalapeños if you want them.
  3. Fold the dough over the filling and pinch the edges closed. Air fry at 180°C for 10–12 minutes, until the outside is golden and the dough is cooked through.

Notes

The filling amounts are up to you; just leave enough bare dough around the edges to seal the bread.
