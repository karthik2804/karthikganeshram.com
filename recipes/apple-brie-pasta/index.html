Apple and Brie pasta

Creamy pasta with lightly caramelised apple, melted Brie, crispy sage, and toasted walnuts.

Cook
20 min
Yield
1 serving

What goes in

Ingredients

  • 100–110 g pasta
  • 70–80 g Brie
  • 1 apple, diced
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 15 g butter
  • 30–40 ml cream
  • 15 g walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 5–6 fresh sage leaves
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • A little pasta cooking water, reserved

I cook the apple just until it turns golden, leaving a little bite alongside the melted Brie.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water. Save a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan and set aside.
  3. Melt the butter in the pan and fry the sage leaves until crispy. Remove and set aside.
  4. Sauté the shallot in the sage-infused butter until softened. Add the diced apple and cook for 3–4 minutes, until lightly caramelised but still firm.
  5. Reduce the heat. Add the cream and Brie, and stir gently until the cheese melts.
  6. Toss in the pasta, adding a splash of reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce. Season to taste.
  7. Finish with the toasted walnuts, crispy sage, and freshly ground black pepper.
