Creamy gochujang udon

Spicy, creamy udon with a silken tofu and gochujang sauce, topped with a fried egg.

Cook
10 min
Yield
1 serving

What goes in

Ingredients

  • 100 g dry udon noodles
  • 150 g silken tofu
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • ½ tsp red miso
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1–2 tsp gochugaru
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp butter
  • 2–3 tbsp noodle water, or as needed
  • 1 egg
  • A little neutral oil, for frying the egg
  • Spring onions, sliced, to finish
  • Sesame seeds, to finish

Inspired by Buldak noodles, but with blended silken tofu making the sauce creamy.

Method

  1. Cook the udon according to the packet instructions. Save a little noodle water before draining.
  2. Blend the silken tofu, gochujang, miso, soy sauce, and honey or sugar until smooth.
  3. Heat the butter and sesame oil in a pan. Fry the garlic and gochugaru briefly, then add the blended sauce. Warm it gently, stirring in 2–3 tbsp noodle water to loosen it.
  4. Toss the cooked udon through the sauce. Meanwhile, fry the egg in a little oil in another pan.
  5. Top the noodles with the fried egg, spring onions, and sesame seeds.

Notes

Start with 1 tsp gochugaru and add more if you want it hotter. Keep the sauce on a gentle heat once the tofu is in the pan.
