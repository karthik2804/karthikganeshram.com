A note from Belgium

Belgium (Ghent, Bruges, Antwerp & Coastal Loop)

Coastal cycle loop from Ghent to Knokke and Bruges, plus exploring Ghent, Antwerp, and Brussels with my parents.

I explored Ghent, Antwerp, and Brussels with my parents. It was a chance to spend time together across a few Belgian cities.

I also made a coastal cycling loop from Ghent to Knokke-Heist, through Bruges, and back to Ghent. That ride is one of the long Belgium days I still remember from 2025.
