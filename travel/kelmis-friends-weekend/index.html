A note from Belgium

A Birthday Weekend in Kelmis

A birthday weekend with Rotterdam friends in Kelmis, including a hike to the three-country point.

June 2025 Holiday

Along the way

Places in this note

  1. 01Kelmis
  2. 02Drielandenpunt

In June 2025, my friends from Rotterdam gave me a weekend away in Kelmis as a birthday gift. We hiked to the Drielandenpunt, where Belgium, the Netherlands, and Germany meet.

We liked the weekend enough to make it a tradition: another friends trip to Belgium the next year, this time in Theux.
