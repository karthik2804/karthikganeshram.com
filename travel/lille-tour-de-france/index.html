A note from France

Lille (Tour de France)

Watching the 2025 Tour de France opener in Lille, my first bike race in person.

5 July 2025 Holiday

I went to Lille for the opening stage of the 2025 Tour de France. It was the first bike race I had seen in person, and Jasper Philipsen won the stage.

We had a barbecue at a friend’s place for lunch before watching the race. That mix of time with friends and the excitement of seeing the Tour live made it a great day.
