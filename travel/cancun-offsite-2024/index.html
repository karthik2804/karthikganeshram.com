A note from Mexico

Cancún with Fermyon, 2024

A Fermyon team offsite in Cancún that turned into a table tennis challenge.

2024 Offsite

I returned to Cancún in 2024 for another offsite with the whole Fermyon team. That year, my teammates discovered I was good at table tennis.

We ended up having tournaments where they tried to beat me. Nobody managed it, and the games became one of my favorite memories of the trip.
