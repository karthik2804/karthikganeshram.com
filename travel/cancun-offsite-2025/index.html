A note from Mexico

Cancún with Fermyon, 2025

A third Cancún offsite with the whole Fermyon team in 2025.

2025 Offsite

In 2025, I went back to Cancún for a third offsite with the Fermyon team. As with the earlier trips, the best part was spending time together with the whole team outside our everyday work routine.

The earlier visits gave me my first try at snorkeling and some undefeated table tennis tournaments. Together, the three offsites are a set of good memories with the team.
