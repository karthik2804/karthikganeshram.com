I use cashews to make the tomato sauce smooth and rich, then finish it with butter and a little cream.

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a pan. Add the cardamom and onion and cook until the onion softens. Add the ginger and garlic and cook for another minute.
2. Add the tomatoes, cashews, and a little salt. Cover and cook until the tomatoes collapse, about **8–10 minutes**. Stir in the chilli powder, garam masala, and coriander, and cook for another 2 minutes.
3. Let the mixture cool slightly, then blend with some of the water until smooth. Strain it if you want a particularly silky sauce.
4. Melt the butter in the pan. Add the blended sauce and enough remaining water to keep it pourable. Simmer gently for **10–12 minutes**, stirring now and then.
5. Add the paneer and crushed kasuri methi. Cook gently for **2–3 minutes** to heat the paneer through. Turn off the heat, stir in the cream, and taste for salt. Finish with coriander.
