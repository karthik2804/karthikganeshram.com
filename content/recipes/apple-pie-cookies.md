The apple filling needs to be cool and thick before it goes into the dough. I make these with either an egg or milk and baking powder, depending on who is eating them.

## Method

1. Cook the diced apple with the butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, cornflour, and lemon juice over medium heat until the apple is tender and the juices are thick, about **6–8 minutes**. Cool completely.
2. Beat the dough butter and sugars until creamy. Mix in the vanilla and **either** the egg **or** the milk. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt; for the eggless version, add the baking powder here too. Mix just until combined, then chill for 30 minutes.
3. Heat the oven to **175°C** and line two baking trays. Divide the dough into 12 pieces. Flatten each piece, add about 2 teaspoons of cold apple filling, and pinch the dough closed around it.
4. Place seam side down with plenty of space between cookies. Bake for **13–15 minutes**, until the edges are set and lightly golden. Let them firm up on the tray for 10 minutes before moving them.

## Egg and eggless versions

Use the egg **or** the milk and baking powder for a batch, never both. If the eggless dough feels soft, chill the shaped cookies for 15 minutes before baking.
