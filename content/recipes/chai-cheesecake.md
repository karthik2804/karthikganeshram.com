Strong tea and chai spices flavor the filling, while whipped cream keeps it light.

## Method

1. Steep the black tea in the boiling water for **8 minutes**. Strain and let it cool completely. You will need about **2 tbsp** of the strong tea.
2. Crush the biscuits finely and mix with the melted butter. Press into the bottom of an **18 cm springform tin** and chill while you make the filling.
3. Beat the cream cheese, icing sugar, cardamom, cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and 2 tbsp of the cooled tea until smooth.
4. In another bowl, whip the cold cream until it holds soft peaks. Fold it gently into the chai cream cheese mixture. Spread over the chilled crust and smooth the top.
5. Cover and chill for at least **6 hours**, preferably overnight, before removing from the tin and slicing.

## Notes

Taste the filling before folding in the whipped cream. Add a little more cardamom if you want the chai flavor stronger.
