Inspired by an Australian lamington: vanilla cookie, chocolate, coconut, raspberry, and mascarpone, all in one.

## Method

1. Simmer the raspberries and sugar for **6–10 minutes**. Mix the cornflour with the cold water, stir it into the raspberries, and cook until thickened. Let the compote cool.
2. Brown the butter until golden and nutty, then let it cool until lukewarm. Mix in both sugars, followed by the beaten egg and vanilla.
3. Fold in the baking powder, baking soda, salt, and **85 g flour**. Add a little more flour if the dough needs it, without making it stiff. Divide into four balls and chill for **20–30 minutes**.
4. Heat the oven to **180°C conventional / 165°C fan**. Bake the cookies for **11–14 minutes**. While they are still warm, press a shallow well into each one. Let them cool completely.
5. Gently mix the mascarpone, icing sugar, and vanilla until smooth, then refrigerate. Lightly toast the coconut in a dry pan and let it cool.
6. Melt the chocolate and coat the tops and sides of the cooled cookies, leaving the bottoms and wells uncovered. Sprinkle with coconut before the chocolate sets.
7. Fill each well with raspberry compote and finish with a small dollop of vanilla mascarpone.

## Storage

Refrigerate once assembled. Best on the day they are made, or within two days.
