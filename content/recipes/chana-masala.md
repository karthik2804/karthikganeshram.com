Using canned chickpeas makes this an easy weeknight curry. Cooked dried chickpeas work too; make sure they are tender before they go into the tomato sauce.

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the onions and green chilli until the onions are golden, about **8–10 minutes**. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
2. Add the tomatoes and a little salt. Cook until the tomatoes are soft and the mixture has thickened. Stir in the coriander, cumin, garam masala, turmeric, and chilli powder; cook for another minute or two.
3. Add the chickpeas and enough water to make a loose sauce. Cover and simmer for **15–20 minutes**. Mash a spoonful of chickpeas into the sauce if you want it thicker.
4. Stir in the kasuri methi and amchur, or finish with lemon juice. Taste for salt and top with fresh coriander.
