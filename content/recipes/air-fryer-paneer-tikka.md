Thick yogurt helps the marinade cling to the paneer and vegetables. The air fryer gives the edges a little char without a tandoor.

## Method

1. Mix the marinade ingredients in a bowl. Gently coat the paneer, onion, and pepper, then leave for at least **20 minutes**.
2. Heat the air fryer to **200°C**. Arrange the pieces in a single layer in the basket, leaving space around them. Cook in batches if needed.
3. Air fry for **5 minutes**. Turn the pieces and brush with any marinade left in the bowl. Cook for another **2–4 minutes**, until the edges are lightly charred and the paneer is heated through.
4. Serve hot with lemon and, if you like, mint chutney.
