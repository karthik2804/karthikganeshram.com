In June 2025, my friends from Rotterdam gave me a weekend away in Kelmis as a birthday gift. We hiked to the Drielandenpunt, where Belgium, the Netherlands, and Germany meet.

We liked the weekend enough to make it a tradition: another friends trip to Belgium the next year, this time in [Theux](/travel/theux-friends-weekend/).
